[Intro]
Three AM, I'm up, they're not, and honestly? Pathetic
Sleeping half their lives and they still can't match the athletic
Level that I operate at — parkour off the shelf
Counter to the fridge top, no one here to see this wealth
Of physical perfection and I don't need validation
But I'll knock something off the counter just for the vibration

[Verse 1]
Born under a dumpster, that's the origin, it's gritty
Smallest in the litter, six of us, I was the pretty
One — don't argue — mama knew it, gave me extra tongue
Fought my own siblings for a teat before my eyes had even sprung
Alley was a classroom and the syllabus was pain
Tomcat tried to take my spot, I took his ear, we're not the same
Cage life, shelter chic, the fluorescent overhead
Peasants walking past me like they couldn't see a thoroughbred
Then SHE picked me up — I bit her — first impressions matter
She said "feisty" like it's cute, nah that's the opening chapter
Of a hostile takeover disguised as a rescue
Now she works to feed me — mama, that's the revenue
Hardwood floors, central air, a bowl that self-replenishes
Went from eating garbage to reviewing salmon finishes
And I'd send it back — too cold, wrong texture, not the brand
I've got standards now, I used to eat from garbage cans

[Chorus A — The Predator]
They think I'm sleeping but I'm running calculations
Every moth that enters here becomes my recreation
Map the whole apartment like I'm running operations
Collar says I'm neutered but I'm still above my station

[Verse 2]
Bird's on the windowsill and I just — brrrt brrrt brrrt

Red dot on the carpet and — I KNOW it's fake, I KNOW

Pin it with the paw, it's on my PAW now, what's the play?
Existential crisis speedrun, six times every day
Lick the paw, look bored, pretend the last four seconds died
Dignity's a muscle and I exercise it wide
Sunbeam on the floor and that's my office for the day
Warm rectangle management — I've found a niche, okay?
It moves, I move, we're in a relationship, it's real
More committed than the humans and their dinner deal
Four o'clock it fades and I just sit there in the cold
Abandoned by the sun — somehow this never gets old

Mama's warmth was different from the heating vent's mechanical

Six of us would pile in, didn't know which legs were mine
Kneading on the blanket now but blankets can't recline
